The Steward's Model

One Master. One steward. Twelve fields.

Nothing you have is originally yours. All of it is on loan, all of it is material for love, and you will be asked about it. The model puts that whole truth on one page: twelve fields, in three rings.

What I am

The four with which we are asked to love God. The self is the first thing entrusted to you.

The Household

What I give

The World

The neighbour, widened to the earth itself. Here the light leaves your hands.

  • Creation
  • Speech
  • Beauty and Making
  • Mission

Think of a parish church. A lamp burns before the tabernacle: Christ, the centre. Around him the temple, which is you. Around the temple the house. Beyond the doors the field. Lamp, temple, house, field.

Each ring has its colour, like the glass of a rose window: ruby for the Person, sapphire for the Household, emerald for the World. Gold is kept for the centre. They are a memory aid, not the Church's liturgical colours.

In every field, four movements

He took

Receive

Name what was given, and give thanks. This was not mine first.

He blessed

Bless

Set it in its place under God. Order the loves.

He broke

Spend

Put it to work at a cost. A talent never risked earns nothing.

He gave

Return

Hand it back: to someone, and to God in the evening.

They echo what the Lord did with bread: he took, blessed, broke and gave. Stewardship is what the weekday brings to the altar on Sunday.

The book

Illuminated: The Image of God, Embodied

You are made in the image of God. That image is embodied, and every part of your ordinary life is the material in which it is meant to shine. Your sleep, your money, your calendar, your speech and your kitchen table are not beside the spiritual life. They are where it happens.

Sixty-two pages, grounded in Scripture and the Catechism of the Catholic Church, with every claim referenced so it can be checked. The text still awaits review by a priest.

The app

Self-knowledge in the presence of God, turned into times and places for love.

Every screen is a chapter of the book in working form. It opens in your browser and can be added to your phone’s home screen. The whole General Roman Calendar is inside, day by day.

It lights, and never scores.

No points and no streaks. A lit petal records a practice kept. It does not measure grace.

One field at a time.

You choose one field for a season of about ninety days. The other eleven stay quiet.

Begin again, without penalty.

A missed day is simply not marked. For hard weeks, floor mode keeps only three lines.

Private by construction.

No account, no tracking. Everything stays on your own device.

The Guide.

Plain questions that turn your answers into a first rule of life and a plan for the day. It follows fixed questions. It is not an AI and not a spiritual director.

The Icon Screen.

A mirror in five panels: the dwelling, the gifts of the Spirit, the charisms, the shadow, the calling. A result is not an identity.

My day.

Today laid out hour by hour, with your anchors, your commitments and the day of the Church’s year.

Into your calendar.

Your Rule as one calendar file for Apple or Google. The app cannot see or change your calendar; you import the file.

Companions

The app does not replace the prayer apps you already use. It links out to Laudate, Divine Office, Ascension and Hallow. These are independent works, and Illuminated Life is not affiliated with them.

Open the app

On a phone: open the app, then choose Share and Add to Home Screen.

A note on authority

This is a private work of formation. It is not an official text of the Church and carries no imprimatur. The text awaits review by a priest. It is meant to agree in every point with Sacred Scripture and the Magisterium, and it is submitted to the Church's judgement. Where it is found to be wrong, it will be corrected.

The app is not a spiritual director, a confessor or a diagnosis.
