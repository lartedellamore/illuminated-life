In every field, four movements

He took

Receive

Name what was given, and give thanks. This was not mine first.

He blessed

Bless

Set it in its place under God. Order the loves.

He broke

Spend

Put it to work at a cost. A talent never risked earns nothing.

He gave

Return

Hand it back: to someone, and to God in the evening.

They echo what the Lord did with bread: he took, blessed, broke and gave. Stewardship is the weekday form of what is placed on the altar on Sunday.

The book

Illuminated: The Image of God, Embodied

You are made in the image of God. That image is embodied, and every part of your ordinary life is the material in which it is meant to shine. Your sleep, your money, your calendar, your speech and your kitchen table are not beside the spiritual life. They are where it happens.

Fifty-seven pages, grounded in Scripture and the Catechism of the Catholic Church, with every claim referenced so it can be checked.

The app

Self-knowledge in the presence of God, turned into scheduled love.

Every screen is a chapter of the book in working form. It opens in your browser and can be added to your phone's home screen.

It lights, and never scores.

No points and no streaks. A lit petal records a practice kept. It does not measure grace.

One field at a time.

You choose one field for a season of about ninety days. The other eleven stay quiet.

Begin again, without penalty.

A missed day is simply not marked. For hard weeks, floor mode keeps only three lines.

Private by construction.

No account, no tracking. What you write is stored only in your own browser.

Open the app

On a phone: open the app, then choose Share and Add to Home Screen.

A note on authority

This is a private work of formation. It is not an official text of the Church and carries no imprimatur. It is meant to agree in every point with Sacred Scripture and the Magisterium, and it is submitted to the Church's judgement. The app is not a spiritual director, a confessor or a diagnosis.
